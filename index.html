How this backtest works
  • Signals are checked when each candle closes. Trades open at the next candle’s open, so the test never uses prices it could not have known at the time.
  • Only one trade is open at a time. With “Both” directions, an opposite signal closes the trade and opens one the other way.
  • Take profit and stop loss are checked against each candle’s high and low. If both are hit in the same candle, the stop loss is assumed to have hit first.
  • Fees are charged on entry and exit. Funding costs and slippage are not included.
  • With leverage, a trade is treated as liquidated when the price moves 1 ÷ leverage against it. Real exchanges liquidate a little earlier.
  • Max drawdown is the largest fall in balance from a previous peak, measured at each candle close.
  • Grid bot: grid lines are evenly spaced. Grids above the starting price buy the coin at the start so they can sell as the price rises. Each grid buys at its lower line and sells at its upper line, without leverage. Inside a candle, the price is assumed to move open, low, high, close on rising candles and open, high, low, close on falling ones.

What gets tested
  • Entry rules: EMA crossover 9/21, 20/50 and 50/200. RSI (length 14) with levels 30/70, 25/75 and 20/80. Breakout of the last 20, 50 and 100 candles. Bollinger Band bounce (length 20) with 2 and 2.5 standard deviations.
  • Each entry rule is tested long only, short only and both, with the 200 EMA trend filter off and on.
  • Exits: opposite signal only; take profit 3% with stop loss 1.5%; take profit 6% with stop loss 3%. The last two also close on an opposite signal. Bollinger rules also try closing at the middle band with a 5% stop loss.
  • Grid bots: 10 and 20 grids, with ranges of ±10% and ±20% around the starting price.
  • Every signal strategy puts its whole balance into each trade. Fees, leverage and all other rules work the same way as in Strategy Backtest.
Prices come straight from each exchange’s public market data. The price at a time is the first trade of the candle that starts then: second-level on Binance spot, minute-level everywhere else. All times are UTC+8. Coinbase prices are in USD, the others in USDT, so they can differ slightly. The Trade Backtest leaves out fees. The Strategy Backtest includes trading fees. Neither includes funding costs or slippage.
