Test a rule-based strategy on past candles. The tool opens and closes trades whenever your rules say so, then shows how the strategy would have done.

Market and period

Dates are in UTC+8, and the end date is included. Smaller timeframes over long periods need many candles and take longer to download.

Entry rule

Direction
Takes longs only when price is above the trend EMA, and shorts only when it is below.

Exit rules

Take profit and stop loss are price moves from the entry price. Leave a box empty to turn that exit off.

Position and fees

Charged on entry and on exit. Typical taker fees are about 0.05% to 0.1%.
How this backtest works
  • Signals are checked when each candle closes. Trades open at the next candle’s open, so the test never uses prices it could not have known at the time.
  • Only one trade is open at a time. With “Both” directions, an opposite signal closes the trade and opens one the other way.
  • Take profit and stop loss are checked against each candle’s high and low. If both are hit in the same candle, the stop loss is assumed to have hit first.
  • Fees are charged on entry and exit. Funding costs and slippage are not included.
  • With leverage, a trade is treated as liquidated when the price moves 1 ÷ leverage against it. Real exchanges liquidate a little earlier.
  • Max drawdown is the largest fall in balance from a previous peak, measured at each candle close.
Prices come straight from each exchange’s public market data. The price at a time is the first trade of the candle that starts then: second-level on Binance spot, minute-level everywhere else. All times are UTC+8. Coinbase prices are in USD, the others in USDT, so they can differ slightly. Backtests leave out fees, funding costs and slippage.
