Coin price tools

Look up a coin price at any moment, or test what a trade would have earned between two times.

Pick a coin and a time to see its price then, plus the high, low and volume for that day.

Choose a coin, buy or sell, and the times you opened and closed the trade. The profit or loss is worked out from the exchange prices at those two times.

Action
Buy earns when the price rises. Sell earns when the price falls.
Prices come straight from each exchange’s public market data. The price at a time is the first trade of the candle that starts then: second-level on Binance spot, minute-level everywhere else. All times are UTC+8. Coinbase prices are in USD, the others in USDT, so they can differ slightly. Backtests leave out fees, funding costs and slippage.
